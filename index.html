About Us

A Journey of Passion,
Expertise & Excellence

Varyzon Cake Shop is a premium, 100% pure vegetarian bakery brand founded by Chef Virendra Sawant, bringing over 25 years of professional experience in the Bakery & Pastry industry. Our passion is to create fresh, delicious, beautifully designed cakes and desserts.
